01

PERSONAL PROJECT

Hip-Hop Archive

2026 +

ABOUT

An interactive digital archive exploring the history and development of hip-hop. The project combines my American Studies research with web development to present hip-hop history through an interactive timeline and artist lineage tool.

TECHNOLOGIES

  • Next.js
  • React
  • TypeScript
  • MusicBrainz API

MY CONTRIBUTIONS

  • Designed and developed an interactive timeline tracing hip-hop from its historical foundations through contemporary movements.
  • Applied research from my American Studies coursework to develop and contextualize the archive's historical content.
  • Integrated the MusicBrainz API to retrieve artist information and discography data.
  • Developed an artist lineage tool that connects individual artists to genres, movements, influences, and moments within hip-hop history.
  • Organized historical events and concepts into structured datasets that can be expanded as the archive grows.
